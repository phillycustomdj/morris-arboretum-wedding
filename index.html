Part One

The Ceremony

Your ceremony at the Arboretum can take place in one of several locations. Each has its own character — and its own sound challenges. We've done them all, many times. Our speakers stay hidden so they don't end up in your photos, and every guest hears the officiant clearly no matter how far back they're seated.

Baxter Memorial

The Baxter Memorial has a brick floor that's easier to stand on — particularly if you're wearing heels. It also makes a beautiful spot for a group photo right after the ceremony.

Rose Garden

The Rose Garden is one of our favorite ceremony spots at the Arboretum — intimate, surrounded by blooms, and gorgeous in photos. We position the sound system to stay out of your sightlines and your pictures.

Pennock Garden

Pennock can host either a ceremony or a cocktail hour. Either way, we run on a battery-powered system tucked off to the side so it doesn't interfere with the view or the photos — and every guest still hears every word.

Rain Plan

If the weather turns, the ceremony moves into the tent. We've done this many times — once everyone's settled, your guests won't even notice the change.
